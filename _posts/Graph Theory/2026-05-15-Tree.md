## Definition
**(i)** A graph is called a ***tree*** if it is connected and has no cycle.

**(ii)** A vertex of degree one is called a ***leaf***.

---
## Theorem
For a graph $T$, TFAE.

**(i)** $T$ is a tree.

**(ii)** For any vertices $u, v \in V(T)$, there is a unique $uv$-path in $T$.

**(iii)** $T$ is connected, but is disconnected by the removal of any edge.

**(iv)** $T$ has no cylce, but has a cycle on the addition of any edge.

**(v)** $\vert V(T) \vert = \vert E(T) \vert + 1$.

---
## Theorem
In any finite tree, there is a leaf. If we remove a leaf, we get a tree again.
