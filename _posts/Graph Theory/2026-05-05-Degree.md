## Definition
Let $G$ be a graph, and let $v \in V(G)$.

**(i)** The ***neighborhood*** $N_G(v)$ of $v$ is the set $N_G(v) = \\{ u \in V(G) \mid u \sim v \\}$.

**(ii)** The ***degree*** $\deg_G(v)$ of $v$ is the size of the neighborhood of $v$, that is, $\deg_G(v) = \vert N_G(v) \vert$.

**(iii)** The ***graph score*** of $G$ is the set $\\{ \deg_G(v) \mid v \in V(G) \\}$. 

**(iv)** The ***degree sequence*** of $G$ is the sequence of the graph score of $G$ in non-decreasing order.

**(v)** A non-decreasing sequence $D$ is said to be ***graphic*** if it is the degree sequence of some graph $G$, which is called a ***realisation*** of $D$. That is, the vertex set $V(G)$ satisfies that $\mathrm{deg}_G(v_i) = d_i$ for each $i = 1, 2, \cdots, n$.

즉 $v$와 인접해 있는 버텍스들의 집합을 네이버후드라고 부르고, 그 네이버후드의 크기, 그러니까 $v$와 인접해 있는 버텍스들의 개수를 $v$의 degree라고 부른다. 그러면 자명하게 모든 버텍스들의 degree의 합은 정확히 edge의 개수의 두 배가 되어야 한다. 

$$\sum_{v \in V(G)} \deg(v) = 2 \vert E(G) \vert.$$

---
## Handshaking Lemma
For any graph $G$, $\displaystyle \sum_{v \in V(G)} \deg_G(v)$ is even.

## Remark
**(i)** Isomorphic graphs have the same degree sequence.

**(ii)** The converse of (i) is NOT true.

$(\because)$ The following two graphs have the same degree sequence $(2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2)$, but not isomorphic.

**(iii)** There is a nondecreasing sequence of nonnegative integers which is NOT a degree seqeunce of a graph.

$(\because)$ Take $(1, 2)$. Since the sum of degrees of a graph must be even, $(1, 2)$ cannot be a degree sequence of a graph.

**(iv)** A realisation of the graphic seqeunce need not be unique.

## Theorem (Havel-Hakimi)
If a sequence $D = (d_1, d_2, \cdots, d_n)$ is graphic, then there is a realisation $G$ of $D$ such that the vertex $v_n$, having the degree $d_n$, is adjacent to $v_{n-1}, \cdots, v _ {n - d_n}$.

## Corollary
Let $D = (d_1, d_2, \cdots, d_n)$ be a nondecreasing sequence. Then $D$ is graphic $\iff$ the sequence $D' = (d_1, d_2, \cdots, d _ {n-d_n-1}, d _ {n - d_n}-1, \cdots, d_{n-1} - 1)$ is graphic.

### Proof
If $D$ is graphic, then there is a realisation $G$ of $D$ such that the vertex $v_n$, with $\mathrm{deg}_G(v_n) = d_n$, is adjacent to $v_{n-1}, \cdots, v _ {n - d_n}$ by Havel-Hakimi Theorem. By removing the vertex $v_n$ from $G$, we get a realisation $G'$ of $D'$. Thus $D'$ is graphic.

Conversely, if $D'$ is graphic, then there is a realisation $G'$ of $D'$. By adding a vertex adjacent to $v _ {n - d_n - 1}, \cdots, v_{n-1}$ to $G'$, we get a realisation $G$ of $D$. Thus, $D$ is graphic. $\blacksquare$
