## Definition
A group $G$ is said to be ***decomposable*** if it is isomorphic to a direct product of two proper nontrivial subgroups. Otherwise, $G$ is called ***indecomposable***.

---
## Theorem 1
The finite indecomposable abelian groups are exactly the cyclic groups with order a power of a prime.

---
## Theorem 2
Let $G$ be a finite abelian group or order $n$. If $m \vert n$, then $G$ has a subgroup of order $m$.

---
## Theorem 3
If $m$ is a square free integer, that is, $n$ is not divisible by the square of any prime, then every abelian group of order $m$ is cyclic.
