## Cauchy's Theorem
If a function $f(z)$ is analytic and $f'(z)$ is continuous at each point interior to and on the simple closed contour $C$, then 

$$\int_C f(z) \, dz = 0.$$

## Cauchy-Goursat Theorem
If a function $f(z)$ is analytic at each point interior to and on the simple closed contour $C$, then 

$$\int_C f(z) \, dz = 0.$$
