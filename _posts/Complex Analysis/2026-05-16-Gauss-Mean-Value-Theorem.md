## Gauss's Mean Value Theorem
Suppose that a function $f$ is analytic inside and on the closed circle $\vert z - z_0 \vert = \rho$, centered at $z_0$ and with radius $\rho$. Then 

$$f(z_0) = \frac{1}{2 \pi} \int_0^{2\pi} f(z_0 + \rho e^{i\theta}) \, d\theta.$$
